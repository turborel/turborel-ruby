# Connecting

[Contents](README.md)

This chapter will tell how a program connects to a server: over TCP, a
Unix socket or a Windows named pipe; as which user, proved by trust, a
password, a certificate or SCRAM-SHA-256; over TLS; within which
timeouts; and how a connection ends, or its statement is cancelled. It
is filled at milestone M2, and the standard API's way of connecting at
M7.
